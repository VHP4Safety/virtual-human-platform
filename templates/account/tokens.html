Using a token

Send the record's fields, named as in schema.org, with the token. A link is written as {"@id": "https://..."}. The record is checked against the platform's model and its page shows it at once.

curl -X PUT {{ request.url_root }}api/tools/my-tool \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer vhp_..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"name": "My tool", "url": {"@id": "https://example.org"}}'

POST /api/tools makes the address from the name, DELETE /api/tools/my-tool removes the record (add ?permanent=true to free its address), and {{ request.url_root }}tools/my-tool.jsonld gives the record as it is saved. Every change is listed with your name on the sources page.
